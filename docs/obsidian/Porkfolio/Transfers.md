# Transfers

[[Pages and Workflows]] · [[Games]] · [[Backups]] · [[Media]]

Transfers is the shared queue for FTP work launched from other Porkfolio pages.

## What it shows

- Queued, active, completed, cancelled, and failed jobs.
- Job type, game association where known, source/destination, progress, transfer rate, and log/error information.
- Aggregate activity visible through the navigation badge and Dashboard state.

## What you can do

- Watch uploads, downloads, folder copies, and deployment tasks started from other pages.
- Pause/resume the queue when supported by the current jobs.
- Cancel a specific job.
- Clear completed entries after reviewing their outcome.

## Operational guidance

Keep this page open during large or destructive operations. A queued job means Porkfolio has accepted the task; it does not guarantee that every remote file was written successfully. Review completion and error details before acting on the result.
