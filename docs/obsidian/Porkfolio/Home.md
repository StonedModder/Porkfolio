## Vault conventions

- Use wikilinks for relationships between notes.
- Keep host-specific paths, credentials, keys, console IPs, tokens, and webhooks out of notes.
- Update the relevant page note whenever a page's user-visible behavior changes.
- The root `README.md` is GitHub-facing; use normal Markdown links there and keep the detailed operational material here.
