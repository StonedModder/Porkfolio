GarlicSaves

Contribute worker time to the save community — completely optional.

What is GarlicSaves?

GarlicSaves is a community-driven project that tracks gameplay statistics across jailbroken PS5 consoles. By enrolling, your console runs a background worker (garlic-worker-ps5.elf) that periodically polls for jobs

Enrollment is completely optional. You can enable or disable the worker at any time from this page.

Start Contributing

Running

Make sure your PS5 is jailbroken and elfldr is loaded on port 9021 before clicking Start. The worker will run until you restart your PS5.

Working…

Start GarlicSaves Worker?

This will upload the config to your PS5 and send the worker payload immediately.

⚠ To stop the worker, restart your PS5. The worker runs in the background until then.

Setup Tips

  • Your PS5 must be jailbroken and elfldr must be running on port 9021 before clicking Start Working.
  • Porkfolio will upload your config.ini to /data/garlic/ on your PS5 (overwriting any existing file), then send the ELF payload via TCP.
  • The worker runs in the background until you restart your PS5. No reboot is required to start — only to stop.
  • Go to Settings → Power Save Settings on your PS5 and disable automatic rest mode so the worker stays running.
  • View your stats and manage your account at garlicsaves.com/contribute.
